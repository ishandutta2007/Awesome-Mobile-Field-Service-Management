# Awesome-Mobile-Field-Service-Management

## Top Mobile Field Service Management Tools Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Mobile-First Dispatch, Offline Job Execution & Technician Productivity*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Mobile Field Service Management Tools**. These tools help service businesses schedule and dispatch technicians, track jobs in real time, capture field data offline, process payments on-site, and connect field operations with back-office systems — all from mobile apps built for the realities of fieldwork.

**Examples** include Salesforce Field Service Lightning, ServiceTitan Mobile, Jobber App, Housecall Pro Mobile, Praxedo Mobile, ProntoForms (TrueContext), FieldAware, BigChange, mHelpDesk, and TrueContext (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom field workflows, and transparent technician data — ideal for service contractors, maintenance teams, and developers building data-sovereign field operations platforms.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce Field Service Lightning](https://www.salesforce.com/products/field-service/)**  
  Enterprise field service management suite within Salesforce with mobile technician app, scheduling, dispatch, and work order management.

- **[ServiceTitan Mobile](https://www.servicetitan.com/)**  
  Mobile field service app for the trades with job scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, and AI-powered technician assistance (Field Pro) [citation:4].

- **[Jobber App](https://www.getjobber.com/features/field-service-management-app/)**  
  Mobile-first field service app for quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and customer communication — trusted by 400,000+ service pros [citation:5][citation:15].

- **[Housecall Pro Mobile](https://www.housecallpro.com/)**  
  Field service management platform with mobile app for scheduling, dispatch, payments, and customer notifications.

- **[Praxedo Mobile](https://www.praxedo.com/)**  
  Cloud-based FSM software with offline-capable mobile app, AI-powered scheduling, and real-time work order tracking for 5–500+ technicians [citation:6][citation:16].

- **[ProntoForms (TrueContext)](https://truecontext.com/)**  
  Low-code mobile workflow platform for field data collection, inspections, and compliance reporting with offline-first architecture [citation:7][citation:17].

- **[TrueContext](https://truecontext.com/)**  
  AI-augmented field service platform (formerly ProntoForms) with mobile forms, guided workflows, and enterprise integrations for 100,000+ technicians [citation:7][citation:18].

- **[FieldAware](https://www.fieldaware.com/)**  
  Field service management software with mobile app, parts/inventory tracking, and NetSuite ERP integration for service contractors [citation:9][citation:19].

- **[BigChange](https://www.bigchange.com/)**  
  All-in-one job management system with mobile app (JobWatch), CRM, scheduling, live tracking, and finance for field service businesses [citation:10][citation:20].

- **[mHelpDesk](https://mhelpdesk.com/)**  
  Field service automation software for HVAC and trades with mobile app for estimates, invoicing, scheduling, and QuickBooks integration [citation:11].

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[FlexDesk](https://github.com/Paulo-BatistaFerraz/flexdesk)**  
  Open-source FSM platform for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and landscaping businesses. Features job management, client CRM, invoicing with Stripe, team scheduling, role-based access, offline-first mobile app (React Native), multi-tenant architecture, and Google Maps routing. Built with NestJS, Next.js, and PostgreSQL [citation:2][citation:13].

- **[SiteTrackr](https://github.com/SuperDaveOrg/SiteTrackr)**  
  Open-source FieldOps maintenance platform for technicians working remote sites. Features dynamic ticket templates, site metadata, grouped custom fields, visit logging, attachments, role-based permissions, and offline-capable PWA. Built with Fastify and React [citation:3].

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  
  Open-source ERP with field service management modules including work orders, scheduling, technician assignment, and customer management. Best for organizations wanting FSM tightly integrated with accounting, inventory, and CRM [citation:12].

- **[openMAINT](https://github.com/openMAINT/openMAINT)**  
  Open-source CMMS/IWMS for facility and asset maintenance. Work orders tied to buildings, equipment, and locations with preventive schedules and asset history. Best for internal maintenance teams rather than contractor dispatch [citation:12].

- **[Budibase](https://github.com/Budibase/budibase)**  
  Open-source low-code platform for building custom internal field-operations apps. Useful for job intake forms, inspection workflows, technician status updates, and approval queues when no packaged FSM fits [citation:12].

- **[OpenBoxes](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes)**  
  Open-source inventory and supply-chain software. Useful as a parts-management layer for field service when inventory accuracy is the bottleneck — stock movement, warehouse, lot tracking, and replenishment [citation:12].

- **[openCRX](https://github.com/openCRX/openCRX)**  
  Open-source CRM with service case and activity management. Can be adapted for customer-driven service workflows, though it lacks modern mobile-first FSM features [citation:12].

- **[LibreWO](https://github.com/librewo/librewo)**  
  Lightweight open-source work-order and customer management for small repair shops and computer service businesses. Simple but narrow in scope [citation:12].

- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**  
  Open-source ERP/CRM with modules adaptable for field service: customer management, proposals, orders, and interventions tracking.

- **[Odoo Field Service](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  
  Open-source ERP with field service module including task scheduling, timesheets, and mobile-friendly work order management.

- **[Tania](https://github.com/Tanibox/tania)**  
  Open-source farm management platform with task tracking and mobile-friendly interfaces adaptable for field operations.

- **[Grocy](https://github.com/grocy/grocy)**  
  Open-source household/grocery management with inventory tracking, task scheduling, and mobile web interface — adaptable for small field teams.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[Frappe Helpdesk](https://github.com/frappe/helpdesk)** — Open-source ticket and field service support system built on Frappe/ERPNext framework.
- **[Zammad](https://github.com/zammad/zammad)** — Open-source helpdesk with ticket routing, team inboxes, and mobile-responsive interfaces for service coordination.
- **[Chatwoot](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot)** — Open-source omnichannel support platform for field team communication and customer updates.
- **[Cal.com](https://github.com/calcom/cal.com)** — Open-source scheduling infrastructure for appointment booking and technician availability.
- **[Rallly](https://github.com/lukevella/rallly)** — Open-source scheduling polls for coordinating field team availability.
- **Offline-First Libraries** — PouchDB, Dexie, WatermelonDB for building offline-capable field apps.
- **Mobile Frameworks** — React Native, Flutter, Capacitor for cross-platform technician apps.
- **Maps & Routing** — OpenStreetMap, Leaflet, GraphHopper, OSRM for job location and route optimization.
- **Payment Processing** — Stripe, PayPal, GoCardless SDKs for on-site payment collection.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **FlexDesk** or **ERPNext** for FSM core, **SiteTrackr** for field ticketing, **OpenBoxes** for inventory, **Cal.com** for scheduling, and **OpenStreetMap + Leaflet** for mapping to build a complete self-hosted mobile field service stack.
